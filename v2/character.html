    Backstory

    Twelve years in, I’ve gone from junior creative to leading user-centred design for NHS England. Somewhere along the way I worked out that the job isn’t making the thing, it’s making the team that makes the thing.

    I’ve built design practices and systems from nothing, run research when we weren’t allowed to talk to users, and shipped experiments that moved commercial numbers. Today I’m pointing all of that at AI: agentic tooling that takes the grind off designers so they can think harder.

    Outside work I co-chair the Yorkshire & North East Powerlifting Federation and design MTFBWY, a tabletop RPG with weekly playtests and a loyal table.
